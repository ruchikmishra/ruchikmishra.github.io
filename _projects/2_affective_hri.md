In my doctoral work at the University of Louisville I developed multimodal emotion recognition for human–robot interaction across neurodiverse populations. This included social robots in interventions for children with autism, personalized speech emotion recognition with vision transformers, and human-mediated large language models for robot-assisted therapy.

Related papers:

- [Personalized speech emotion recognition using vision transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.10687), IEEE RA-L 2025
- [Human-mediated large language models for robotic intervention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00260), arXiv 2024
- [Social impressions of the NAO robot and its impact on physiology](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10388187), ACIIW 2023
- [Towards forecasting engagement in children with autism](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10115150), IEEE SoutheastCon 2023
